## 4. The profiles as this model implements them

A profile sets three things and nothing else. `same-traffic` checks on the network files the simulator got that every case of one fan-in differs only in the lines `pfc`, `ecn`, `cc_mode` and `seed`. `config-applied` reads every value back from the model objects after setup.

| Profile | `QbbEnabled` on every port | switch `EcnEnabled` | RdmaHw `CcMode` |
|---|---|---|---|
| c-none | false | false | 0 |
| c-pfc | true | false | 0 |
| c-ecn | false | true | 1 |
| c-both | true | true | 1 |

What each switch does in this model, from the code at `3e0c7c1`:

- **PFC.** The switch's MMU sends PAUSE when an ingress queue crosses `(buffer - headroom - reserve - shared) >> pfc_alpha_shift` and RESUME when it drains (`switch-node.cc:92-132`, `switch-mmu.cc:93`). It does so in every profile. `QbbEnabled` decides only whether a host acts on a received PAUSE (`qbb-net-device.cc:492`). So "PFC off" means the hosts ignore PAUSE, not that the switch stops sending it. A host that acts stays paused until a RESUME arrives. It never lets a PAUSE expire after its pause time (`qbb-net-device.cc:494-496`). With PFC off, a packet that finds the ingress threshold and its headroom full is refused by the MMU (`switch-mmu.cc:38`).
- **ECN.** With `EcnEnabled` the switch marks CE at dequeue with probability 0 at or below kmin, `pmax × (q - kmin) / (kmax - kmin)` between them and 1 above kmax, where q is the MMU's egress bytes (`switch-mmu.cc:100-108`, `switch-node.cc:207-214`). The receiver sets the CNP flag in the ACK of a CE-marked packet. This model carries no separate CNP packet for CC mode 1.
- **DCQCN.** In `CcMode` 1 a CNP-flagged ACK starts the Mellanox DCQCN of `RdmaHw` (`rdma-hw.cc:549`, `833-898`). `CcMode` 0 matches none of the model's branches (1, 3, 7, 8, 10), so no congestion control runs and a CNP flag changes nothing.

A fair PFC on/off comparison is therefore possible only in one sense. It compares hosts that obey PAUSE with hosts that ignore it, behind a switch whose admission and PAUSE generation are the same in both. A pure lossy switch that uses its whole buffer and sends no PAUSE does not exist at this commit. The minimal change would give `SwitchNode` an attribute that skips `CheckAndSendPfc` and `CheckAndSendResume` and lets `CheckIngressAdmission` admit up to the free buffer. No second patch was made. That needs the coordinator's decision (section 15).

The counters come from trace sources: `QbbPfc` for the handler, `PhyTxBegin` and `MacRx` for frames and CE bits, `QbbEnqueue` and `QbbDequeue` at the switch, and the QP's `m_rate` sampled every 1 us. Across all 200 cases of `c-03` to `c-06`, `pfc-as-configured` and `ecn-as-configured` found the following.

- With PFC on, the handler ran once for every PAUSE and RESUME, and the MMU refused no packet.
- With PFC off, the handler never ran, although hosts received PAUSE frames.
- With ECN off, there was no mark, no CNP flag and no rate change.
- With ECN on, no mark fell at or below kmin, and every dequeue above kmax was marked. Between kmin and kmax, 2.1% to 12.3% of dequeues were marked in the 90 ECN-on cases where the queue sat there, below pmax 0.2 as the linear curve requires.

### Different code paths

`profiles-differ` compares, for each fan-in and seed, two profiles that differ in one feature on the same traffic. When the feature's trigger occurred in either case, the two must give different flow results and counters. A PAUSE sent by the switch triggers PFC. A bottleneck egress queue above kmin triggers ECN. In `c-03`, 20 PFC pairs and 30 ECN pairs saw their trigger, and all gave different results. Ten pairs saw no trigger: c-ecn against c-both at fan-in 2 and 4. There DCQCN kept the queue below the PFC threshold, no PAUSE was sent, and the two profiles gave byte-identical outputs. They took the same path because nothing triggered PFC, which the check allows and reports. In `c-04` 25 PFC and 30 ECN pairs saw their trigger and 5 did not, c-ecn against c-both at fan-in 2. In `c-05` 10 PFC and 20 ECN pairs saw their trigger and 30 did not: at fan-in 2 the peak queue was 68380 B, below kmin, and all four profiles took the same path, and at fan-in 4 and 8 DCQCN kept c-ecn and c-both below the PFC threshold.

## 5. DCQCN parameters

The values come from SimAI's `astra-sim-alibabacloud/inputs/config/SimAI.conf` at `a57d765`, the configuration SimAI ships with this model. They are set on every `RdmaHw` and read back in `config-applied`.

| `RdmaHw` attribute | Value | SimAI.conf line | Model default |
|---|---|---|---|
| `RateAI` | 50 Mb/s | 21 `RATE_AI` | 5 Mb/s |
| `RateHAI` | 100 Mb/s | 22 `RATE_HAI` | 50 Mb/s |
| `MinRate` | 100 Mb/s | 23 `MIN_RATE` | 100 Mb/s |
| `EwmaGain` | 0.00390625 (1/256) | 19 `EWMA_GAIN` | 1/16 |
| `RPTimer` | 900 us | 18 `RP_TIMER` | 1500 us |
| `RateDecreaseInterval` | 4 us | 16 `RATE_DECREASE_INTERVAL` | 4 us |
| `AlphaResumInterval` | 1 us | 15 `ALPHA_RESUME_INTERVAL` | 55 us |
| `FastRecoveryTimes` | 1 | 20 `FAST_RECOVERY_TIMES` | 5 |
| `ClampTargetRate` | false | 17 `CLAMP_TARGET_RATE` | false |
| `RateOnFirstCnp` | 1.0 | not set | 1.0 |

The other values of the shared incast network: MTU 1000 B, buffer 4194304 B, PFC pause time 5 us, headroom 3 × rate × delay per port (37500 B) and alpha shift 3 as in M5, and ECN kmin 400000 B, kmax 1600000 B and pmax 0.2, SimAI.conf's values for 100 Gbps (line 52 onward). The buffer is SimAI.conf's `BUFFER_SIZE 32` (MiB) divided by 8. With 32 MiB the PFC threshold of a fan-in of 8 lies near 2 MB per sender, above every queue these 1 MB flows build, so PFC would never trigger. With 4 MiB it lies at 0.4 MB per sender at fan-in 2 and 0.24 MB at fan-in 8, inside the range the workloads reach. This is a choice for the comparison, not a recommended switch buffer.

The rate trace of flow 0 in `n8-c-ecn-s1` shows what these values do (`raw/run1/out/work/n8-c-ecn-s1/rates.tsv`). The rate halves every 4 us from 27 us to 63 us, from 100 Gbps to the 100 Mb/s `MinRate` floor. Alpha starts at 1 on the first CNP and decays by only 1/256 per microsecond (`rdma-hw.cc:819`), so each cut halves the rate (`rdma-hw.cc:863`). Fast recovery waits for the 900 us `RPTimer` after the last cut (`rdma-hw.cc:868`). The rate stays at 100 Mb/s until 1051 us, then jumps to 50.05 Gbps, halfway to its target (`rdma-hw.cc:898`). This explains the long FCTs of the ECN profiles below.

## 7. Hypotheses and what the model did

Each profile's expected effect was a hypothesis. These are the outcomes for this model, these parameters and these workloads, not general properties.

| Hypothesis | Outcome in this model |
|---|---|
| Without control the queue grows and packets are lost | Yes wherever the queue passed the PFC threshold and the headroom. c-none refused 60 to 9962 packets per case and resent up to 11385. In `c-05` at fan-in 2 the queue peaked at 68380 B and nothing was lost |
| Lost packets are recovered | Not always. The model has go-back-N on NACK and no retransmission timer. A NACK needs a later packet to reveal the gap. In `c-04` at fan-in 8 the late sender's tail was refused whole, after 305000 B, so no NACK came and the flow never finished. This happened in all five seeds of c-none and four of c-ecn (`tests/fixtures/c-ns3/incast/c-04-incast-staggered/n8-c-none-s1/`) |
| PFC makes the network lossless | Yes for these cases. Every PFC-on case, c-pfc and c-both, refused no packet. The headroom of 3 × BDP held the in-flight data |
| PFC is faster | In this model, for these workloads, c-pfc had the lowest median large-flow FCT and the highest goodput (93.0 to 94.7 Gb/s) of the four profiles in every case with congestion. That is a result of these parameters. Against it, c-none's drops and go-back-N cost 2% to 18% of the median large-flow FCT |
| ECN with DCQCN keeps queues short and avoids loss | Partly. It lowered the peak queue at fan-in 2 and 4. At fan-in 8, and at 4 in the staggered workload, c-ecn still refused 349 to 3110 packets per case, and in `c-03` at fan-in 8 its peak queue of 2363844 B exceeded c-none's 2242864 B. ECN with DCQCN is not lossless here |
| ECN with DCQCN improves completion | No for large flows. With SimAI.conf's DCQCN values a sender's rate can fall to the 100 Mb/s floor within 36 us and recover only after the 900 us timer (section 5). The median large-flow FCT was 2.3 to 5.7 times c-pfc's, and goodput fell to 15.6 to 44.9 Gb/s. Yes for small flows in `c-05`: their median FCT fell from 120 us (c-none) or 115 us (c-pfc) to 90 us at fan-in 8, and from 53 us to 42 us at fan-in 4 |
| PFC and ECN together combine the benefits | They combine losslessness with DCQCN's rate collapse. c-both refused no packet and paused far less than c-pfc (42 to 48 PAUSE against 190 at fan-in 8), but its FCTs follow c-ecn's. Where DCQCN kept the queue under the PFC threshold, c-both was the same as c-ecn |

## 12. What the results mean and do not mean

- **They are model behavior.** Every figure is what ns-3-alibabacloud `3e0c7c1` with one patch computes for these inputs. It says nothing about a ConnectX NIC, a switch ASIC or a production network, and the link rates are model inputs, not comparable with Track A's RXE bandwidth.
- **The parameters decide the ranking.** c-pfc came first and the ECN profiles last here because SimAI.conf's DCQCN values collapse the rate to 100 Mb/s for 900 us. Other DCQCN values, another buffer or another workload can order the profiles differently. Only kmin was varied, in the optional `c-06`.
- **"PFC off" is a host setting.** The switch still sends PAUSE and keeps PFC headroom accounting in every profile (section 4). A comparison against a switch without PFC needs a model change that was not made.
- **Loss recovery is incomplete.** Without a retransmission timer a refused tail stalls a flow forever. The completion ratio of c-none and c-ecn reflects that gap of the model, not a property of RDMA transports.
- **Few flows.** A fan-in of 2 gives 10 large flows over five seeds and a fan-in of 8 gives 40. The maxima and medians above are of that many flows. No figure is a tail percentile.
- **Simulator cost is not network performance.** Wall time and RSS are kept as `simulator.*` metrics. One 60-case run took about 35 s.
