The reference pages come from the repository itself at build time, so they cannot fall behind it.

- [Make targets](/reference/make-targets/) reads the `##` comments of `Makefile` and `mk/*.mk`, the same lines `make help` prints.
- The repository documents below are copied from `docs/` by `scripts/sync-docs.mjs` at every build. Their own links become site links, a code span that names another copied document becomes a link to it, and paths under `.lab/`, its evidence copies in `.lab/evidence/` among them, and `results/` stay code, because those files exist only on the machine that ran the lab.

## Running and proving the lab

| Document | What it covers |
|---|---|
| [runbook](/docs/runbook/) | From a fresh host to every track, with measured durations and sizes, cleanup, recovery and the safety rules |
| [verification](/docs/verification/) | How the lab proves itself, the latest full reproduction and the MVP checklist with its evidence |
| [troubleshooting](/docs/troubleshooting/) | Failures seen in every track and the cel1 simulation, with their causes and evidence |
| [fleet](/docs/fleet/) | The cel1 region, its fleet targets and its simulation |
| [setup-vmware](/docs/setup-vmware/) | The same lab as VMware Workstation guests, not verified on the current host |
