Each concept page teaches the ideas a set of labs relies on, shows them in a real output of the lab, and ends with its primary sources. Read the page of a track before its labs.

| Page | Ideas | Labs that use them |
|---|---|---|
| [RDMA, verbs and Soft-RoCE](/concepts/rdma/) | Verbs, queue pairs and memory regions, RDMA CM, Read, Write and Send, the RoCEv2 frame, GIDs, RXE | Track A |
| [Kubernetes networking](/concepts/kubernetes-networking/) | CNI, Cilium in tunnel mode and with kube-proxy replacement, kube-vip, Multus, Whereabouts, macvlan, hostNetwork pods with RDMA devices | Track A, cel1 |
| [InfiniBand management](/concepts/infiniband-management/) | The subnet manager, GUIDs and LIDs, SMPs and MADs, port states, minhop and updn, rail-optimized fabrics, ibsim | Track B |
| [RoCE congestion control](/concepts/roce-congestion-control/) | Lossless Ethernet, PFC and headroom, ECN with kmin, kmax and pmax, CNPs and DCQCN, incast, FCT and its bounds | Track C |

The concept pages describe the mechanisms in general and then say how the lab's tools implement them. Where the two differ, the lab's behavior is what its runs measured. For example, the ns-3 model's switch sends PFC PAUSE frames even when PFC is "off", and the RXE device reports placeholder firmware fields because no hardware exists.
