Short flows suffer most from long queues, because their own transfer is brief and the wait dominates. c-05 mixes the two kinds. The first ceil(N / 2) senders start one flow of 1000000 bytes at 0. Each other sender starts flows of 16000 bytes at 10, 30, 50 and 70 us, into the queue the large flows built.

## Goal

Compare the four profiles on mixed traffic, with the checks of [c-03](/labs/track-c/c-03-incast-simultaneous/), and report the small-flow FCT apart from the large-flow FCT.

## Concepts it uses

- [Incast](/concepts/roce-congestion-control/), [ECN marking and DCQCN](/concepts/roce-congestion-control/), [PFC](/concepts/roce-congestion-control/).
- [FCT and its bounds](/concepts/roce-congestion-control/). A small flow came closest to its bound here, at 1.14 times.

## What the model did

- **At fan-in 2** the peak queue was 68380 B, below kmin. Nothing marked, nothing paused, and all four profiles took the same path and gave identical rows.
- **Small flows** finished faster under ECN with DCQCN. Their median FCT fell from 120 us under c-none and 115 us under c-pfc to 90 us at fan-in 8, and from 53 us to 42 us at fan-in 4.
- **Large flows** finished slower under ECN with DCQCN, as in c-03. Their median rose from about 360 us under c-pfc to about 1213 us at fan-in 8.
- At fan-in 4 and 8 DCQCN kept c-ecn and c-both below the PFC threshold, so the two rows are identical.

## Break it yourself

No break ran on c-05 in the records. Its configuration sets `run_breaks: false`, because it uses the check code of [c-03](/labs/track-c/c-03-incast-simultaneous/), whose breaks failed every incast check.

## Questions to think about

- Small flows gained from DCQCN while large flows lost. Which part of DCQCN helps a 16000 B flow that never gets a CNP itself?
- At fan-in 2 the four profiles agree exactly. What does `profiles-differ` require in that case, and why is agreement correct there?
- Would a fairer comparison give small and large flows different priorities? What would that test that this one does not?

## What the result means and does not mean

The run shows a trade between short and long flows in this model with SimAI.conf's DCQCN values.

> [!WARNING]
> Model behavior for these parameters and this workload, not hardware. Other DCQCN values, another buffer or another workload can order the profiles differently. 20 large flows at fan-in 8 and 80 small ones are the samples, so no figure is a tail percentile.
